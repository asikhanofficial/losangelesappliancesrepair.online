Professional Oven Repair in Los Angeles

Restoring precise baking temperatures, reliable broiler ignition, and convection performance for gas and electric wall ovens.

Precision In-Home Wall Oven Repair

An accurate oven temperature is critical for successful cooking and baking. Whether you are roasting meats or baking delicate pastries, a 25-degree temperature deviation or an intermittently failing heating element disrupts meal preparation.

At Los Angeles Appliances Repair, we service electric wall ovens, convection units, and gas oven cavities, calibrating heating cycles and repairing electronic controller components safely.

Common Oven Symptoms We Resolve

  • Uneven Baking & Temperature Drift: Baked dishes burning on the bottom while the top remains raw, caused by a burned bake element or drifting temperature probe.
  • Oven Fails to Heat: In electric ovens, bake or broil elements fail to glow red. In gas ovens, the glow bar igniter glows dimly but never opens the safety gas valve.
  • Oven Door Won't Close Securely: Worn hinge assemblies causing the heavy glass door to hang loose, allowing heat to escape and scorch cabinet surfaces.
  • Error Codes (F-Codes) on Control Panel: Digital error codes signaling sensor resistance faults or relay contact failures.
  • Self-Clean Latch Stuck Locked: Motorized door latch remains jammed in locked position after running a self-cleaning cycle.

Our Diagnostic Approach

Ovens require rigorous testing to confirm safety and heat calibration:

  • Thermal Sensor Resistance: Measuring RTD oven sensor ohms at ambient temperature to ensure factory curve calibration.
  • Heating Element Inspection: Testing bake and broil elements for physical blister spots, breaks, or internal open circuits.
  • Gas Igniter Draw: In gas ovens, measuring igniter amperage with a clamp meter (typically 3.2 to 3.6 amps required to trigger the bi-metal gas safety valve).
  • Relay Board Testing: Verifying that control board relays switch line voltage properly under load without arcing.

Common Oven Parts Repaired or Replaced

  • Bake & Broil Heating Elements: 240V heavy-gauge resistance coils producing radiant baking heat.
  • Hot Surface Igniters: Silicon carbide or silicon nitride elements that ignite natural gas burners safely.
  • Oven Temperature Sensors: Micro-ohm sensors communicating cavity temperature back to the control processor.
  • Door Hinges & High-Temperature Silicone Gaskets: Trapping heat inside the cooking cavity.
  • Convection Fan Motors: Circulating forced hot air for balanced multi-rack baking.

Why Professional Repair Makes Sense

Wall ovens are mounted inside custom cabinetry with high-voltage 240V hardwired feeds or gas lines. Replacing an oven often requires custom woodworking and cabinet alterations. In-home repair maintains your existing kitchen aesthetic and restores original heating calibration at minimal cost.
