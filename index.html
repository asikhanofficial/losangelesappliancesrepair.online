Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about appliance repair and what to expect during a service visit.

We service all major household appliances including refrigerators, freezers, dishwashers, washing machines, clothes dryers, ovens, cooking stoves, and ranges. Both freestanding and built-in configurations are supported.

Key signs include unusual grinding or buzzing noises, water pooling beneath the unit, failure to heat or cool to set temperatures, burning smells during operation, error codes on digital displays, or cycles stopping mid-operation. Addressing these symptoms early prevents secondary damage.

Ensure clear physical access to the appliance by clearing items off top surfaces and out of the immediate surrounding floor space. For refrigerators or ovens, note the model number (typically located on an interior door frame or back panel) to help the technician identify replacement specifications.

Refrigerators and washing machines operate with motors, pumps, and continuous cycles, making them frequent candidates for servicing. Dishwashers with drainage issues and dryers with heating element burnout are also very common home service requests.
